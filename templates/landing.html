How it works

  1. Download a CSV from your bank or expense app.

    It needs five columns: Date, Category, Amount, Type and Description.

  2. Upload it to FinSight.

    The file is read in memory and never saved on disk. Uploading the same rows again adds nothing twice.

  3. Read your overview.

    Or load 8 months of sample data first and look around.
